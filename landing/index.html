Official IELTS Speaking Band Descriptors Calibration (FC, LR, GRA, P)

PairTalk evaluates and calibrates partner matching based on the four official British Council / IDP IELTS Speaking criteria:

Fluency and Coherence (FC)
Measures your ability to speak at length with natural flow, minimal hesitation, appropriate linking phrases, and coherent discourse sequencing.
Lexical Resource (LR)
Evaluates your vocabulary breadth, precision, idiomatic expressions, collocations, and ability to paraphrase without repetition.
Grammatical Range and Accuracy (GRA)
Assesses your mastery of compound and complex sentence structures, tense consistency, and error-free clause construction.
Pronunciation (P)
Scores phonetic clarity, syllable stress, sentence rhythm, intonation patterns, and effortless intelligibility.

PairTalk supports strict whole-band scoring across Band 5, 6, 7, 8, and 9, matching candidates with complementary strengths for maximum mutual improvement.

Complete 3-Part Exam Format Simulation

Every PairTalk voice call is structured to mirror the official 2026 IELTS Speaking test format:

  1. Part 1 — Introduction & General Interview (4–5 mins): Familiar questions on home, work, studies, hobbies, and everyday experiences.
  2. Part 2 — Individual Long Turn Cue Card (3–4 mins): 1 minute of silent preparation followed by an uninterrupted 2-minute speaking monologue on a prompt card.
  3. Part 3 — Two-Way Abstract Discussion (4–5 mins): In-depth thematic questions exploring societal, philosophical, and analytical perspectives linked to the Part 2 topic.

Partners alternate roles as examiner and candidate, giving both participants authentic test-day experience from both perspectives.
