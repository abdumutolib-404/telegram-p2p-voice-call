# PairTalk Privacy & Payment Policy

**Effective Date:** August 30, 2026

Welcome to **PairTalk**. We are dedicated to providing a safe, private, and effective environment for IELTS Speaking practice. This document outlines our data privacy practices, audio handling principles, and our official refund & cancellation policies.

---

## 1. Information We Collect & Privacy Protection

### A. Candidate Profile & Anonymity
- **Permanent Alias**: Each student is assigned a permanent anonymous identifier (e.g. `P2P-0284DB68`). Your real phone number, name, and personal contact info are never shared with other learners.
- **IELTS Band & Skills**: Self-reported target band and subscores (Fluency, Vocabulary, Grammar, Pronunciation) used exclusively to match you with suitable speaking partners.
- **Telegram Account ID**: Numeric account identifier used solely to authenticate your account and save your progress.

### B. Voice Call Privacy
- **Live Practice Calls**: Real-time voice calls are encrypted and transmitted directly for live practice. Live calls are never monitored, tapped, or listened to.
- **Practice Recordings (Optional)**:
  - Audio recording is completely optional and activated only if a participant turns it on during a session.
  - Recorded audio is private and accessible only to the participants of that specific session.
  - Recordings are automatically deleted from cloud storage when their retention period expires:
    - **Free Plan**: 1 day retention
    - **Plus Plan**: 7 days retention
    - **Pro Plan**: 30 days retention
    - **Boss Plan**: 90 days retention

---

## 2. Refund & Cancellation Policy

### A. 100% Refund Eligibility
You are eligible for a full refund if **BOTH** of the following conditions are met:
1. The refund request is submitted within **48 hours** of purchase; **AND**
2. You have used **less than 10%** of your purchased monthly practice call allowance.

### B. Ineligible Circumstances
Refunds cannot be issued if:
- More than 48 hours have elapsed since the transaction; OR
- You have utilized 10% or more of your monthly call credits; OR
- The account was suspended or permanently banned due to severe violations of Community Guidelines (e.g., harassment, hate speech, or abuse).

### C. Refund Processing Methods
- **Telegram Stars (XTR)**: Eligible Star payments are refunded automatically back to your Telegram balance via `/refund` in the bot. Your plan will be immediately returned to the Free tier.
- **UZS Card Payments**: Requests submitted via `/refund` or support are reviewed by payment administrators. Approved refunds are returned to the originating payment card within 1–3 business days.

## 3. Account Deletion & Data Rights

- You have the right to request the complete deletion of your account, call statistics, and active recordings at any time.
- To request account data erasure, contact support directly through `@PairTalkSupport` or use `/support` in the Telegram Bot.
