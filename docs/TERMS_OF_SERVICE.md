# Terms of service — repository draft

Status: documentation draft reviewed 2026-10-05. It replaces obsolete implementation claims in this checkout, not approved customer terms. The public landing maintains its own content; publication and legal review are separate work.

## Service and accounts

PairTalk provides peer English/IELTS speaking practice through Telegram. It is independent of official examination bodies, issues no official score/certificate, and does not guarantee a band improvement, matching time, or partner availability.

Account access requires valid Telegram authentication. Safeguard your account and do not create abusive duplicate accounts, manipulate rewards, or automate disruption. The previous draft proposed a minimum age of 13 with guardian permission for minors; deployment-specific eligibility and consent requirements still need owner review before final publication.

## Conduct and recording

Follow [Community guidelines](COMMUNITY_GUIDELINES.md) and [Safety](SAFETY_GUIDE.md). Harassment, solicitation, misuse of private information, and unauthorized recording/redistribution are prohibited.

Current automatic moderation uses warnings, six-hour temporary suspensions, and permanent account suspension at the documented thresholds. Appeals are available. Do not promise permanent partner blocking or universal immediate review.

Discuss permission before recording. Account acceptance is separate from informed permission for a specific recorded session. Recordings have finite access/retention and downloaded copies cannot be recalled by server deletion.

## Current purchases and refunds

Current offers use configurable Free/Plus/Pro/Boss subscriptions and account overrides. Show the applicable quantity, duration, retention, currency, and exact total before purchase. Telegram Stars and the manual UZS payment workflow are distinct.

Current refund code permits eligibility when purchase age is under 48 hours **or** usage is below 10%; both conditions are not required by that implementation. Eligibility, ownership, purchase state, and provider/manual processing still apply. Refund confirmation can be delayed and must not be advertised as instant.

Refunding an unapplied incompatible invoice or an old purchase must not revoke a separate valid entitlement. Review all published refund wording against the backend before a release; this paragraph documents code and does not approve a new refund contract.

Current subscription usage is not a lifetime credit ledger. Independent non-expiring calls/recordings, matched optional 30-day storage, and one-time delivery are [planned](unit-pricing.md) and require implementation and approved terms.

## Finalization requirements

Before publication, confirm the operating entity, support contact, jurisdiction/eligibility, purchase rules, privacy notices, service limitations, licensing, and any liability terms. Do not backdate acceptance. The versioned PDF and bot consent workflow are now implemented; [Bot and dashboard](BOT_AND_DASHBOARD.md) documents the current artifact and acceptance audit. This repository draft is not the PDF offered to customers.

This repository draft deliberately removes unsupported guarantees and does not invent a new effective date, liability cap, erasure deadline, or compliance certification. The current technical state is documented in [Architecture](ARCHITECTURE.md) and [Operations](OPERATIONS.md).
