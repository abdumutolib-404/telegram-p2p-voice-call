4. **Security bounty hunter.** Inventory network/user entrypoints and trace remotely reachable authentication, ownership, payment, upload, storage, webhook, crawler and signaling paths to meaningful sinks. Supabase/blockchain examples are not applicable to this app. Verify financial idempotency, duration accounting, recording ownership/expiry and notification consent under concurrency.
