# PairTalk Community Guidelines & Moderation Policy

**Document Version:** 2026.2.0  
**Effective Date:** August 2026  
**Applicability:** All PairTalk Users Across Telegram Mini App & Web Platform  

---

## 1. Preamble & Core Community Mission

**PairTalk** exists to provide an inclusive, safe, and academically rigorous peer-to-peer environment for IELTS Speaking candidates worldwide. Language acquisition requires genuine psychological comfort and vulnerability; our community standards are designed to ensure every learner can practice without fear of harassment, hostility, or exploitation.

Every registered candidate agrees to uphold these standards unconditionally upon entering the matchmaking queue.

├─────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────────┬─────────────────────────────┤

### 1.1 Strict English-Only Communication
All voice communications during matchmaking sessions must be conducted exclusively in English. Switching to native or regional languages defeats the primary pedagogical objective of developing spontaneous English thinking reflex.

### 1.2 Universal Mutual Respect
Candidates hail from over 80 countries. Discrimination, mockery of accents, derogatory remarks about nationality, ethnicity, gender, sexual orientation, religion, or background will result in immediate disciplinary action.

### 1.3 Dedicated Academic Focus
Sessions are strictly reserved for IELTS Speaking simulation and constructive linguistic feedback. Do not use PairTalk for casual dating, social flirting, commercial advertising, or proselytizing.

### 1.4 Reciprocal Feedback Integrity
After each call, participants must submit honest, fair whole-band scores (5 to 9) and qualitative tags. Retaliatory, malicious, or fabricated rating submissions are monitored by our fraud detection heuristics and penalized.

## 3. Article 2: Prohibited Conduct & Zero-Tolerance Violations

The following behaviors are strictly forbidden across all voice rooms, text messaging, and support channels:

1. **Harassment & Bullying**: Repeated insults, personal attacks, intimidation, or aggressive behavior toward speaking partners.
2. **Explicit, Vulgar, or Sexually Inappropriate Language**: Any sexual remarks, unsolicited intimate inquiries, or vulgar profanity.
3. **Queue Manipulation & Ghosting**: Repeatedly entering the matchmaking queue and instantly disconnecting (<10 seconds), dodging partners, or farming referral calls.
4. **Commercial Solicitation & Scams**: Promoting external paid courses, unauthorized tutoring services, crypto schemes, or unauthorized third-party links.
5. **PII Harassment**: Pressuring partners to disclose their real names, phone numbers, social media handles, or physical addresses.
6. **Impersonation & System Fraud**: Impersonating PairTalk staff, falsifying payment proofs, or exploiting software vulnerabilities.

## 5. Article 4: In-Call Reporting & Evidence Preservation

1. **In-Call Report Button**: Tapping the red **Report Partner** button in the voice room immediately prompts you to select the violation type (*Harassment*, *Non-English Speech*, *Commercial Solicitation*, *Explicit Content*).
2. **Emergency Disconnect**: Reporting automatically terminates the call and blocks the offending user from ever being matched with you again.
3. **Telemetry Capture**: When a report is filed, the system securely logs timestamp metadata, signaling disconnect reason, and anonymous participant aliases for administrative auditing.

---

## 6. Article 5: Formal Appeals Procedure

Candidates whose accounts have been suspended or permanently banned possess the right to administrative appeal:

- **How to Submit**: Send `/appeal <detailed reason>` directly to the official Telegram Bot (`@PairTalkBot`).
- **Review Protocol**: Appeals are queued directly in the Admin Operations Console and audited by human moderators within **24 to 48 hours**.
- **One-Appeal Rule**: Only one active appeal can be pending per account at any given time.
- **Irreversible Decisions**: Rejection of a Tier 3 appeal following formal human review is final and unappealable.
