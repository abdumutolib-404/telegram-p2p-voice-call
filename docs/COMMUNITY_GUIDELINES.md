# PairTalk Community Guidelines & Practice Rules

**Effective Date:** August 16, 2026

Our mission at **PairTalk** is to provide an encouraging, high-quality, and respectful environment for IELTS Speaking practice. All learners are expected to adhere to these community standards.

---

## 1. Core Principles

- **Respect & Courtesy**: Treat every speaking partner with respect regardless of nationality, accent, gender, or background.
- **Dedicated Practice**: Focus on speaking English and practicing IELTS topics. Do not use the service for unsolicited commercial promotions, solicitation, or unrelated broadcasting.
- **Constructive Feedback**: Provide constructive, helpful IELTS feedback across criteria (Fluency, Vocabulary, Grammar, Pronunciation) when rating your partner after a call.

---

## 2. Prohibited Behavior

The following actions are strictly prohibited and will result in disciplinary action:
1. **Harassment, Bullying, or Hate Speech**: Any derogatory comments, discriminatory remarks, or hostile behavior.
2. **Explicit or Offensive Content**: Sharing inappropriate, sexually explicit, or offensive language during calls.
3. **Spam & Flooding**: Rapid queue spamming, intentional instant disconnects, or exploiting matchmaking.
4. **Impersonation & Fraud**: Pretending to be an administrator or falsifying payment receipts.

---

## 3. Moderation & Enforcement Policy

We employ automated rating monitoring and administrative moderation to maintain quality:

- **Temporary Suspension (Warning Threshold)**:
  - If a user receives repeated low ratings (<2.5★) or multiple harassment reports, the system automatically issues a temporary 24-hour timeout.
- **Extended Suspension (Second Tier)**:
  - Continued violations result in a 7-day restriction.
- **Permanent Account Ban**:
  - Severe misconduct (hate speech, scams, fraud) results in a permanent ban from all matchmaking and practice features.

---

## 4. Appeals Process

- Permanently banned users may submit a formal unban appeal directly within the Telegram Bot using the `/appeal <reason>` command.
- Appeals are reviewed by human moderators within 24–48 hours.
- Each user is permitted exactly one pending appeal at a time.
