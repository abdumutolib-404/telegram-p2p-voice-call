# PairTalk product upgrade implementation plan

Status: active implementation, not a completion report. Baseline: `f9371b7` on development `origin/main`. Preserve the existing sites, production remote, paid balances and payment snapshots. No rollout until the complete change passes review and the final security gate.

## Scope and decisions

The complete objective covers landing and Mini App polish, one comparison table, configurable unit pricing and durations, a reviewed checkout modal, channel pack offers, channel-gated starter gifts, lifecycle notifications, contextual FAQs, updated policies and downloadable policy documents, final security review, cleanup and publication to development main.

Use the explicitly requested **Buy credits** button as an entry action. Elsewhere use plain calls, recordings, retention and balances. The latest explicit button request overrides the earlier request to remove that word everywhere.

Corrected arithmetic:

- `baseStars = ceil(base15MinuteUzs / starUzsReference * (1 + starMarkupPercent / 100))`. Defaults: 255 UZS/Star and 25% markup; therefore calls cost 18 Stars and recordings 21 Stars, before optional retention. The owner's example describes a markup, not the literal equation `O/X = 1.25S`.
- A 30-minute session uses two 15-minute units: 7,000 UZS / 36 Stars per call and 8,400 UZS / 42 Stars per recording. Round the 15-minute service price first, then multiply by two, as requested. Retention count equals selected recordings; its price and storage allocation scale with recorded duration. Explain this in pricing FAQs.
- `originalPack = callsCost + recordingsCost + optionalRetentionCost`; `discountedPack = originalPack * (100 - discountPercent) / 100`. Calculate UZS and Stars separately, round the final payable amounts upwards, and allow a 100% discount to become a free claim instead of a zero-value invoice.
- No arbitrary purchase quantity ceiling. Accept positive safe whole counts, require recordings <= calls, and constrain only actual payment-provider limits, numerical safety and available recording minutes. Make capacity shortages explicit, not a disguised product maximum.

Telegram's digital-service payment rules require Stars in bots and Mini Apps. The owner explicitly chose to retain the UZS/manual-DM route after being informed of this platform-policy conflict. Implement the requested reviewed UZS flow and show truthful payment status; do not silently replace it with a price-only quote. Source: https://core.telegram.org/bots/payments-stars.

## Design plan

Keep the existing Manrope family and PairTalk palette: dark ink `#111B1D`, sunken ink `#0B1214`, forest `#225B47`, mint `#8DE6C3`, light paper `#F4F7F5`, and light text `#EDF3EF`. Reuse existing light/dark semantic variables rather than introduce another theme. Use left-aligned headings, restrained surfaces and clear spacing. Avoid decorative labels, unnecessary cards and animation on ordinary controls.

The purchase view has a concise service/rate summary, duration and quantity controls, retention option, an itemised running total and one Checkout action. Checkout opens a proper accessible modal showing both totals, expiry/terms, the selected pack and the currency actions. The client should feel native to the existing dashboard. Keep payment progress, cancellations, unavailable capacity and stale quotes reviewable.

Combine communities and tutoring into one four-column comparison: feature, PairTalk, communities, tutoring. Compare matchmaking, level matching, prompts, scheduling, starter practice and tutoring costs. Do not imply communities always charge money or tutors universally lack trials. Keep dated, attributed examples and honest distinctions between peer practice and teaching.

### Channel-offer implementation details

- Use a separate public pack campaign/claim model. Keep legacy invoice settlement and payment history, retire creation of random-user campaigns and suppress their unsent promotional jobs. The new public post opens the main Mini App with `startapp=offer_<campaign UUID>`; it does not issue offer DMs.
- Campaigns freeze the selected pack, price catalog and percentage. Counts are paid claims; checkout holds also count against actual allocatable inventory. Show paid remaining count plus temporary holds clearly. A unique campaign/user claim owns one current order, allows safe recovery of an expired unapproved attempt, and becomes permanently consumed on fulfillment. Refunds do not reopen the person's campaign claim.
- Lock order is User -> PackCampaign -> CreditOrder -> shared recording capacity. Every Stars authorization/settlement and UZS receipt/approval for campaign orders follows that order. Authorizations hold a slot; later expiry does not confiscate an already authorized payment. Free claims fulfill under the same inventory lock, without a zero-value Telegram invoice.
- Channel posting uses durable per-campaign leases and revision reconciliation. A lost send acknowledgement becomes UNCONFIRMED and requires the admin to link the existing channel message; never blindly resend an uncertain publication. Edits/deletes target a known message and may be safely reconciled. Inventory is disabled before cleanup. Remove claim buttons even if deletion fails and show the cleanup state to the admin.
- Offer validity is limited to 47 hours so normal scheduled cleanup stays within Telegram's 48-hour deletion window. Display that API constraint beside the validity input. A prolonged bot outage or revoked permission can still prevent deletion; retain an explicit manual-cleanup state instead of claiming the message disappeared.
- New profiles begin with zero starter units. Existing accounts are marked as already granted without changing balances. The server verifies actual membership with an administrator bot before atomically granting three units once. Leaving/rejoining cannot grant another gift.

### Optional notification implementation details

- Extend NotificationJob with a category and server-generated context; keep transactional receipts/invitations separate from optional reminders. Reuse its persistent delivery worker, outcome states, namespace and durable dedupe keys.
- New optional inactivity, checkout, peak-activity and channel/question-update preferences start off. Show explicit controls in Account and a bot shortcut/stop action. Quiet hours default to 22:00–08:00 Asia/Tashkent; one optional reminder per person per 24 hours, with stricter category cooldowns. Preferences never prevent requested payment receipts or necessary account notices.
- Track last user activity separately from background/admin `updatedAt` changes. Schedule inactivity only after 72 hours, once per inactive episode; abandoned checkout only while its unapproved order is still payable (after 10 minutes, before its 30-minute expiry). Peak notifications depend on actual compatible activity rather than invented time-of-day promises. Topic/channel updates are opt-in, and discount offers remain channel-only.
- Recheck restrictions, current consent/preferences, quiet hours, activity, active calls and order/announcement relevance before sending. Defer quiet-hour jobs; skip those that became paid, cancelled, expired, opted out or irrelevant. Failures with an uncertain Telegram acknowledgement are not automatically resent.
- Provide an admin category preview/history and a reviewed channel-announcement action with idempotency and persistent recipient progress. Reject known offer-post IDs for private announcement delivery. Reuse the actual peak/topic sources instead of running duplicate senders. Pending checkout notifications must resume the existing owned order rather than silently creating a second purchase.
