# Speaking-practice safety

Use your PairTalk alias and keep personal details private. Avoid sharing your address, phone number, passwords, payment card data, or verification codes. Do not pay a partner or follow an unsolicited payment link.

## During a call

- Leave if a partner is abusive, asks for private information, or makes you uncomfortable.
- Discuss recording before starting it and respect a refusal.
- Report the relevant session from dashboard History & audio with a truthful description. Use /appeal in the bot for a permanent suspension.
- Do not download or redistribute someone else's recording without permission.
- Keep account and device access secure; an alias does not prevent a person from recognizing your voice.

The service has moderation and saved-recording access checks. It does not guarantee zero personal-data exposure, permanent pair blocking, an immediate human response, or secrecy from the media provider.

## Recordings

Active recording status and saved access are different. Stopping recording preserves the saved owner's access; it does not erase the delivered audio. Download only through authorized app/bot flows. Temporary signed URLs should be treated as private.

Retention is finite and based on current account/session rules. Deletion failures retry; previously downloaded or forwarded copies cannot be removed by deleting the server object.

## Support

Contact the configured support account through the bot. Do not send passwords, OTPs, or full payment details in a public group. For account sanctions, use the existing appeal workflow.
