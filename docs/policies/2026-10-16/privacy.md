# Privacy policy

Version 2026-10-16. Effective 16 October 2026.

Effective 16 October 2026. PairTalk uses account and session information to provide speaking practice, fulfil purchases and respond to safety concerns. This policy explains that processing and your choices.

Published page: https://pairtalk.online/privacy

## Account and practice data

The service stores your numeric Telegram ID, generated alias, self-assessed bands and preferences, subscription status, and practice activity. Call metadata includes participants, timing, duration, status, ratings, and submitted feedback or reports. Payment records support billing and refunds.

Partners are shown your PairTalk alias rather than your Telegram contact information. The public statistics page exposes aggregate figures, not account identities, private comments, recordings, or payment details.

We also keep purchased call, recording and retention balances, checkout totals and status, campaign claims, starter-gift eligibility, notification preferences, last activity and notification delivery outcomes. These records prevent duplicate charges, gifts and claims, recover interrupted work and apply your messaging choices. We check your channel membership through Telegram when you request the starter gift; joining does not subscribe you to private promotional messages.

## Voice calls and optional recordings

Voice calls use WebRTC transport encryption and media infrastructure. Transport encryption is not a promise of end-to-end encryption inaccessible to the media service.

Cloud recording is optional and requires agreement. Purchased recordings have optional 30-day storage from creation. Without storage, a server copy is kept only for delivery recovery, up to 24 hours, and may be removed after all owners receive it in Telegram. Legacy Free, Plus, Pro and Boss recording entitlements retain their original retention rules. Check History for the file's exact availability. Expired recordings are scheduled for deletion and cannot be restored after removal.

## Service infrastructure

Telegram provides account authentication and the Mini App environment. LiveKit infrastructure handles calls, PostgreSQL and Redis support service state, and configured object storage supports recordings. Hosting and security infrastructure process technical requests needed to operate the website and app.

Do not assume that a partner alias makes everything you say anonymous. Keep identifying information out of conversations and avoid publishing another person's audio without permission.

Hosting, media delivery and storage providers may process information outside your country. Telegram receives messages and delivered audio under its own privacy policy. Private calls are not represented as public question-library data. Do not submit confidential information in speaking prompts, reports or support messages.

## Access, deletion, and support

You can view your current account settings, plan, and call history in the app. The existing policy provides for requests to access or delete account data through @PairTalkSupport (https://t.me/PairTalkSupport) or the bot's billing support command, /paysupport. Its support team will explain the request and any verification needed.

Contact support for questions about a recording, payment record, or data request. Do not post private account information in a public channel.

Necessary account, payment, fraud-prevention and dispute records may need to remain after account closure for the purposes for which they are required. Deleting PairTalk data does not delete files already delivered to Telegram, downloaded by another participant or retained by that participant. Ask support about a specific record or request.

## Payments and refunds

A full unused purchase can be refunded within 48 hours if the complete purchased quantities remain available and unreserved. Use /refund in Telegram. Eligible Stars refunds return the original payment. Existing subscription purchases keep their original 48-hour or less-than-10%-used eligibility rules and applicable account restrictions. Supported legacy card refunds are reviewed and ordinarily settle within 1-3 business days.

See the subscription terms (https://pairtalk.online/terms#payments) and contact billing support for your specific transaction.

New UZS pack payments are manually verified. Request a refund through /refund from the paying account. While a full unused-purchase refund is reviewed, its quantities are reserved and cannot be spent. Confirmation completes the refund; a rejected refund restores that reservation once. A provider's pending or uncertain response is not a completed refund. Contact /paysupport before submitting a second payment or another refund for the same transaction.

Free gifts and zero-price offers have no payment to return. Partial pack refunds are not automated. Purchase and account checks apply, but this policy does not remove mandatory rights under applicable law. Processing time can depend on the payment provider and review; we do not promise instant settlement.
