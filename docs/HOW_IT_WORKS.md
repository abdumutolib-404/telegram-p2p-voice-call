# How PairTalk works

PairTalk is an independent peer speaking-practice service accessed through its Telegram bot and Mini App. It does not issue official IELTS scores or certificates.

## Start a practice session

1. Open the Mini App from the bot so it receives signed Telegram launch data.
2. Set your speaking preferences and self-reported criteria.
3. Join matchmaking. Waiting depends on available compatible participants; there is no guaranteed matching time.
4. Allow microphone access and join the LiveKit room.
5. Use the question drawer for Part 1, cue-card, or Part 3 practice. Agree on roles and timing with your partner.
6. End the session when finished and use the dashboard's rating and report tools when appropriate.

Practice preferences and peer feedback are not examiner assessments. A call is not automatically a fully administered IELTS exam.

## Timing and allowances

Current subscription configuration controls calls, recordings, and retention. The server verifies available allowance; client plan labels do not override it. The authoritative call clock begins at server admission, including connection setup. A qualifying completed session consumes allowance once; delayed readiness cannot reset the timer. Genuine short failures and cancellation retain the existing free handling.

See the actual offer in the bot before buying. The independent-credit system in [unit-pricing.md](unit-pricing.md) is planned, not available.

## Recording and access

Recording is optional and has its own eligibility and active-state controls. Discuss it with your partner before recording; general account terms are not a substitute for informed permission. Do not assume the current backend implements a separate two-person consent ceremony.

Stopping recording stops active intent and preserves saved owner access. A file may become available after provider finalization. Download access checks participation, saved ownership, expiry, and current entitlement. Stored audio is not permanent: cleanup follows applicable retention and retries failed deletion.

## Safety and recovery

Use an alias, avoid sharing personal information, leave uncomfortable calls, and report relevant incidents through the bot. Reports follow moderation rules; they do not prove an automatic permanent pair-blocking feature exists. Reconnect can recover an active session within the current grace behavior; a completed call cannot be revived.
