# PairTalk FAQ

## Is PairTalk an official IELTS service?

No. It provides peer practice, question prompts, and feedback. Self-reported bands and partner ratings are not official examiner results.

## How quickly will I find a partner?

That depends on available compatible learners. There is no guaranteed wait time or guaranteed partner quality.

## Can I use it directly in a browser?

Public guides are readable in a browser. Protected Mini App features require a valid signed Telegram launch. Opening the app directly does not authenticate an account.

## What does a call cost?

Current subscriptions and any approved account overrides control allowance and duration. Check the bot's current offer and total before payment. Stars and UZS offers must be explained in their own payment contexts; a universal fixed dollar/Stars conversion is not assumed. Flexible independent credits are still [planned](unit-pricing.md).

## When does the call timer start?

The server uses admission time, including connection setup. Readiness and reconnection do not reset the allowance. Completed calls meeting the current five-second threshold consume allowance once; genuine short failures and cancellation remain free.

## Can I record a session?

When your account is eligible, use the recording control and discuss permission with your partner first. Recording has separate active-state and saved-owner rules. Stopping does not erase permission to a saved private recording. Provider processing may delay availability.

## Are recordings permanent?

No. Current plan/override retention and access rules apply. Failed deletes remain tracked for retry. Download before access ends; a retention deadline is not a promise of immediate successful deletion during an outage.

## Is audio end-to-end encrypted?

The service uses LiveKit for audio transport. Transport protection must not be described as secrecy from the media/recording provider. Optional recordings necessarily create stored audio. See the [privacy draft](PRIVACY_POLICY.md) for repository-level data notes.

## What if a partner behaves badly?

Leave the call and report the incident through the bot. Moderation and appeals exist; do not assume reporting provides permanent pair blocking or an unconditional free long call. See [Community guidelines](COMMUNITY_GUIDELINES.md).

## How do refunds work?

Use /refund or support for your purchase. Current code evaluates eligibility using purchase age under 48 hours **or** usage below 10%; older Markdown incorrectly required both. Refunds depend on provider confirmation or manual review and are not guaranteed instant. This describes implementation, not a new approved policy. See [Operations](OPERATIONS.md) before publishing revised terms.

## Do referrals and contests always award credits?

Rewards depend on qualifying activity and current contest/referral rules. Joining alone is not a guarantee of an immediate award.

## What if I cannot hear the other person?

Check microphone permission, call volume, and the audio-unlock control; try headphones. Rejoin through the app's supported recovery flow rather than opening an unrelated room. Physical-device troubleshooting is in [Cross-device audio](WEBRTC_CROSS_DEVICE_AUDIO_GUIDE.md).
