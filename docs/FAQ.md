## 1. Core Platform & Matchmaking

### Q1: What is PairTalk and how does live IELTS Speaking matchmaking work?
**Answer:**  
PairTalk is an autonomous peer-to-peer IELTS Speaking preparation platform operating natively inside Telegram and modern web browsers. Candidates select their target whole-band scores (5, 6, 7, 8, 9) across the four official IELTS assessment criteria: Fluency & Coherence (`FC`), Lexical Resource (`LR`), Grammatical Range & Accuracy (`GRA`), and Pronunciation (`P`). When you tap **Start Practicing**, PairTalk’s sub-3-second Redis matchmaking radar pairs you with an active, criteria-matched peer worldwide in an encrypted WebRTC voice room.

---

### Q2: Who is PairTalk designed for?
**Answer:**  
PairTalk is designed for serious candidates preparing for the **IELTS Academic** or **IELTS General Training** examination who need daily, spontaneous speaking practice. It is particularly valuable for:
- Self-studying learners who lack native English speaking environments.
- Candidates struggling to break through the Band 6.0/6.5 ceiling to reach Band 7.0, 7.5, 8.0, or 8.5.
- Learners frustrated with unreliable study partners or expensive 1-on-1 private tutors ($20–$50/hour).

---

### Q3: Why is PairTalk better than searching for IELTS study buddies in Discord servers or Telegram group chats?
**Answer:**  
Public group chats, Reddit forums, and Discord servers suffer from severe inefficiencies: unresponsive partners, frequent ghosting, misaligned language proficiency, background noise, and lack of privacy. PairTalk solves these problems through:
- **Zero Waiting Time**: Instant matchmaking in $<3$ seconds.
- **Calibrated Skill Pairing**: Matching based on whole-band criteria rather than random chance.
- **Structured Timers**: In-call 1-minute cue card prep and 2-minute countdown timers.
- **100% Anonymity**: Permanent `P2P-XXXXXXXX` aliases protecting your personal identity.

---

### Q4: How does PairTalk utilize the official IELTS Speaking Band Descriptors (FC, LR, GRA, P)?
**Answer:**  
PairTalk aligns directly with the official British Council / IDP IELTS Speaking Band Descriptors. Learners configure individual target scores for each criterion (5 to 9). The matchmaking algorithm calculates an overall band score and can pair candidates with complementary strengths (e.g., matching a candidate needing vocabulary improvement with a partner strong in Lexical Resource) for maximum mutual learning synergy.

---

### Q5: Can I practice IELTS Speaking Part 1, Part 2 (Cue Card), and Part 3 (Discussion) on PairTalk?
**Answer:**  
Yes. Every PairTalk call is structured to mirror the complete 3-part exam format:
- **Part 1**: Introduction and warm-up questions (4–5 minutes).
- **Part 2**: Individual Long Turn with an on-screen cue card, automated 1-minute note timer, and 2-minute speaking countdown.
- **Part 3**: Two-way analytical discussion exploring abstract societal themes.
Partners can alternate roles (Examiner vs. Candidate) across consecutive topic rounds.

## 3. Privacy, Anonymity & Safety

### Q8: Is PairTalk completely anonymous and how is candidate privacy protected?
**Answer:**  
Yes, 100%. Every learner is assigned a permanent cryptographic alias (e.g. `P2P-0284DB68`). Your real name, phone number, Telegram `@username`, avatar photo, and IP address are never exposed to other learners. All live voice streams are encrypted end-to-end via WebRTC DTLS-SRTP, and optional cloud audio recordings are automatically purged when their retention window expires.

---

### Q9: What should I do if my speaking partner is unresponsive, abusive, or refuses to speak English?
**Answer:**  
You have total control during live calls:
1. Tap the **Report & Disconnect** button at any time.
2. Select the infraction reason (*Harassment*, *Non-English speech*, *Commercial solicitation*, *Spam*).
3. The partner is immediately blocked so you are never matched again, the session is terminated with zero penalty to you, and our moderation engine logs the incident for review.

## 4. Technology, Setup & Rewards

### Q10: Do I need to download or install any external software to use PairTalk?
**Answer:**  
No external installation is required. PairTalk runs natively inside Telegram as a Telegram Mini App across iOS, Android, macOS, Windows, and Linux. It is also accessible directly via modern web browsers at [pairtalk.online](https://pairtalk.online).

---

### Q11: What audio equipment and permissions are required for PairTalk?
**Answer:**  
- **Hardware**: Any smartphone, tablet, or computer with a working microphone. For best audio fidelity and echo prevention, wired or Bluetooth headphones with a built-in microphone are strongly recommended.
- **Permissions**: Grant microphone permission when prompted by Telegram or your web browser. PairTalk’s auto-unlock audio engine handles mobile audio context activation automatically.

Active learners also compete on the live Community Leaderboard during monthly Speaking Sprints to win free PLUS, PRO, and BOSS plan upgrades.
